## About

I build systems that help people manage complex work: define product experiments, continue tasks across devices, and make unclear processes easier to examine.

At T-Bank, I worked on backend and data-platform components for cashback personalisation. A central project was a declarative experiment model: audiences, conditions and actions are expressed as configuration, while code handles validation and execution. Analysts can prepare some changes without modifying application logic. My work also included Airflow pipelines, Kafka-based data delivery and observability.

In my personal project CODY, I connect an iPhone voice and mobile interface to coding and terminal sessions on a Mac. It is a working prototype built around shared context, a typed protocol and Bridge-side action validation.

As part of the BPMN.space team, I worked on a hackathon prototype for analysts: documents and notes become the basis of an editable process map, while questions and assumptions remain visible beside the result.

My interests are backend engineering, data systems and practical AI tools. I am a first-year undergraduate at Central University. I want to keep developing through work that requires understanding a domain, choosing an architecture and turning it into behaviour that can be checked.
