### ROUTE /learning

Learning how systems work, in depth.
I am a first-year undergraduate at Central University in Moscow, studying programming, mathematics and business subjects while gaining practical software development experience.

I am interested in connecting formal models to the way people actually work. In a banking platform, that means experiment rules. In BPMN.space, it means turning an ambiguous description into an explicit process. In CODY, it means managing state and context across a phone and a computer.

### What I want to learn next
I want a stronger foundation in algorithms, distributed systems and the evaluation of AI components. I also want to get better at connecting an engineering choice to a testable hypothesis: what should improve, how to check it and which limitations remain.

### How I learn through projects
I start with a specific use case, build a small working version, investigate failures and refine the model. In a team, I value clear questions, visible intermediate results and an honest distinction between my contribution and other people's work.

### ROUTE /collaborate

Let's build a useful tool for a specific problem.
I am interested in small products and internal tools that reduce repetitive manual work. I can help with backend services, APIs, data processing and integrations, then connect them to an understandable interface.

For paid work, I suggest starting with one bounded workflow: define the inputs and exceptions, build a pilot and test it against agreed examples. The pilot description sets out the scope and delivery conditions.

For a joint project, I value a clear problem, explicit responsibilities and an early result we can inspect together. Developer tools, data workflows and interfaces to AI systems are particularly interesting to me.

### Tell me about the problem
What outcome do you need? How is it done today? What would a good result look like? These three answers help us find a useful starting point.
