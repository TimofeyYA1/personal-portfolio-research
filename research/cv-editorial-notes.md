# CV editorial decisions

The CV presents backend and data-system work through concrete contributions. The short version prioritises professional project work, CODY and the team BPMN.space prototype. It avoids treating the length of programming practice as continuous commercial employment.

## Dates and role

Earlier and later career materials disagree about the employment timeline. Until the owner confirms the official role names and dates, the text uses **Selected work, 2026** and a functional description of the work. This is a project period, not a hiring date or a replacement official title. The CV is an editorial draft for review; an application version should use confirmed employment details. [E02]

## Results and attribution

- Experiment configuration, data delivery, validation and observability are described as bounded contributions within a team. No claim is made to have created or led the entire platform. [E03–E11]
- Unfinished reuse and refactoring work is not presented as completed delivery. [E06–E07]
- Numerical speed improvements, activity totals and changed-test-file ratios are excluded as proof of impact. No independent measurement is implied. [E12–E13]
- CODY is a personal prototype, with targeted implementation checks and unverified end-to-end readiness. Conflicting documentation is not resolved by guessing. [E16–E18]
- BPMN.space remains a team prototype. Its architecture is a team result; the individual's component ownership requires clarification. [E19]
- Additional client projects are withheld where contribution or public scope is unresolved. Concept work retains its concept label. [E21–E24]

## Qualifications and practical details

Current study is presented as study in progress, without an awarded degree or invented graduation result. Unconfirmed additional enrolment and GPA are omitted. Competition results are self-reported and need certificate checking before an application relies on them. Language proficiency is a self-assessment; relocation intent is not a claim of permission to work in another country. [E25–E28]

Before sending an application, confirm official employment dates and titles, relevant qualifications, the current language statement and availability. Before expanding a case study, confirm personal contribution, collaborator credits and the permitted level of project disclosure. These checks concern specific unresolved claims; they are not evidence that a qualification or contribution is false.
