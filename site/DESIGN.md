# Timofei — connected work

This replaces the rejected first visual direction. It follows the user's Continue style guide while retaining a separate personal identity.

## Tokens
Canvas `#08080a`; raised surface `#141416`; text `#ffffff`; secondary text `#b5b5c0`; action `#7147ee`; light accent `#e8e0ff`. Manrope is self-hosted, including Cyrillic. Display 53–156 px, section 32–56 px, body 16–18 px, useful labels at least 12 px. Tracking no tighter than -0.04em.

## Composition — personal introduction, revision 3
The first screen belongs to Timofei: a large two-line name, specific role, short personal introduction and four explicit destinations — GitHub, LinkedIn, PDF resume and experience. Name occupies the larger left column; the smaller right column explains what he does. A four-link strip spans the width below them. No necessary destination is hidden in an icon or hover.

CODY moves into its own project exhibit, where its spatial illustration supports the case rather than becoming the identity of the whole portfolio. Audience routes follow the introduction. A compact experience/education section names T-Bank and Central University with links to the underlying case and learning route. Long case analysis still follows the relevant local demonstration.

## Interaction
One scene changes with native scroll progress; text and links stay fixed in normal document flow. CODY state changes respond to explicit controls. The large name has a short, local spring response to a fine pointer, restoring immediately when reduced motion is enabled; it is decoration, not an input or information gate. No section-by-section entrance animation. A visible reduced-motion control complements the operating-system preference. On mobile, shorten spatial movement, use normal flow, keep controls large.

## Character
Specific engineering decisions replace abstract slogans. The signature is a pair of open, connected frames, not the Continue product's logo. Purple means a selected state or action; it is not a wash over every heading. No invented metrics, endorsements or production screenshots. Synthetic interfaces are labelled illustrations.

## Why this fits
The first rejected screen hid the person behind an abstract consultancy slogan. Revision 2 named him but let the CODY device scene dominate. Revision 3 takes the owner's Jacek Jeznach reference as a cue for a more personal introduction and responds to the explicit request for visible professional links. The palette remains grounded in the owner's Continue guide. Typography carries the identity, while each project retains its own evidence and status.

## Additional reference: Harnessly
The owner supplied [Harnessly](https://harnessly.ru/). Its large type, visible GitHub destination and layered product explanation informed this revision. In CODY’s exhibit, selecting Task gently separates the two devices and highlights the target terminal; selecting Result highlights the returning phone context. Labels and captions remain readable in normal flow. This borrows the explanatory principle, not its interface, assets or extended scroll sequence.
