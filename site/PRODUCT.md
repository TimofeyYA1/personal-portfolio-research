# Personal portfolio

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Engineering teams evaluating professional work; academic reviewers evaluating projects and motivation; potential collaborators discussing a concrete shared problem. These three audiences were selected in the original brief.

## Product Purpose
Introduce Timofei Iakovlev through his work and make a relevant next conversation easy. The user explicitly wants an expressive, animated website, CVs and research published to GitHub.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Static RU/EN pages, three audience routes, three case studies, local interactive illustrations, CV downloads and contact links. Preserve the existing Site identity and owner-private access. The research/source repository is public. No external messages or analytics are sent by the demos.

## Evidence on Hand
`site-content.json` holds reviewed career and case content. T-Bank is team professional work. CODY is a personal prototype. BPMN.space is a team hackathon prototype. No new titles, employment dates, users, revenue, measured speedups, awards, testimonials or individual authorship may be inferred. Current end-to-end behavior of original projects is not established by this portfolio.

## Brand Commitments
The user rejected the first cream/serif concept and supplied the Continue style guide as a reference, with explicit freedom to extend it. Professional name remains Timofei Iakovlev / Тимофей Яковлев. Language should be direct, concrete and human.

## Product Principles
Show the person and the work early. Make personal contribution explicit. Let interaction explain a real decision. Keep demo values distinct from production claims. Support direct routes for each audience without fragmenting the biography.
